Baccarat Dealer Trainer - Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Baccarat Dealer Trainer (the “App”) handles information in the planned ad-supported production version of the App.

Baccarat Dealer Trainer is an educational training application for learning baccarat dealing rules. It does not offer real-money wagering, prizes, or casino accounts.

1. Information stored on your device

The App may store training information locally on your device, including training scores and accuracy, streaks and completed-hand counts, app preferences, and whether the App has confirmed an active “Remove Ads” entitlement.

This local information is used to operate the App and provide training progress. Local data may be removed if you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App, although a Google Play purchase can generally be restored after reinstalling by checking your Google Play purchase entitlement.

2. Analytics

The production version of the App is intended to use analytics services such as Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and improve reliability and training features.

Depending on the analytics provider, device, operating system, and configuration, analytics data may include:

  • App interactions and feature usage
  • Session and engagement information
  • App version and device/operating-system information
  • Crash, performance, and diagnostic information
  • Pseudonymous app or device identifiers
  • Approximate geographic information inferred from network information, where provided by the service

Analytics information is used to measure feature usage, identify bugs, improve training modes, and understand overall App performance.

3. Advertising

The free version of the App is intended to display advertising using an advertising provider such as Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads.

Depending on your device, settings, consent choices, and the advertising provider's configuration, advertising services may process information such as:

  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • IP address and general device information
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and interactions
  • App usage information related to advertising
  • Diagnostic and fraud-prevention information
  • Approximate geographic information inferred from network information, where applicable

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your location, consent choices, device settings, age-related settings, and applicable law. Where required, the App will request consent or provide privacy choices before serving personalized advertising.

4. Remove Ads purchase

The App is intended to offer a one-time approximately US$2 “Remove Ads” in-app purchase. The final price is the price displayed by Google Play in your country or region.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. The App developer does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account information. Google Play may provide the App with information needed to verify and restore the purchase, such as the purchased product identifier, purchase status, and a purchase token or entitlement status.

Purchasing Remove Ads disables advertising in the App after the purchase is successfully verified. Removing ads does not automatically disable analytics unless the App separately provides an analytics opt-out or applicable law requires otherwise.

5. Service providers and disclosure of information

The App may use third-party service providers to provide analytics, advertising, purchase processing, crash reporting, fraud prevention, and related technical services. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies and the configuration used by the App.

The App developer does not sell personal information for money. However, personalized advertising can involve disclosures that may be treated as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information under some privacy laws. Where required by applicable law, the App will provide the required consent or opt-out choices.

6. Data retention

Locally stored training information remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Analytics and advertising providers may retain information according to their own retention policies and the settings configured by the App developer. Google Play retains purchase records according to Google's policies and legal obligations.

7. Your privacy choices

  • Choose whether certain ads are personalized when a consent prompt is provided
  • Change advertising/privacy settings through your Android or Google account settings
  • Purchase Remove Ads to stop advertising from being displayed in the App
  • Clear locally stored training data through the App or Android settings
  • Contact the developer to ask privacy questions or exercise rights available under applicable law

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If the App is distributed to audiences subject to additional age-related requirements, the App's advertising and analytics configuration should be adjusted accordingly.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information associated with the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of the policy will be updated when material changes are made.
